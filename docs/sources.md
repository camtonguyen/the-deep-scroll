## Dive computer (temperature and sunlight)

Temperature, sunlight and pressure in the dive computer are **rough averages**, not measurements. Pressure is computed from depth. The temperatures (28 °C at the surface, ~15 °C at 200 m, ~4 °C at 1,000 m, ~2 °C deeper) are typical open-ocean values, in `src/shared/lib/readout.ts`; they vary by place and season. We did not find a single NOAA page that states the 1,000 m and deep values; the [World Ocean Atlas](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/70446) holds the data to check them against. The sunlight steps (sunlight to 200 m, dim to 1,000 m, dark below) follow the standard ocean zones.
